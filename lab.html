Springs, not keyframes

Springs, not keyframes

Mini Yaz's head and body follow you with critically damped springs, the fastest kind that never overshoots. Move around the pad. The blue dot is a spring; the gray one restarts a 400 ms ease every time the target moves, so it lurches whenever you change direction.

Stiffness (how fast it catches up)

Each frame: x += (v + w·d)·dt·e−w·dt, with velocity carried over, so a new target bends the path instead of restarting it.
