Tiny Foodie Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 5, 2026

Tiny Foodie is a parent-facing, local-first app. No app account is required. Your child's name and birth month, saved recipes, tried-foods profile and verdict history are stored on your device. Some features send the information described below to our services and providers. Local-first does not mean that nothing leaves your phone.

What leaves your phone

Menu and dish photos, dish text and recipe content. When you scan a menu, check a dish, import a recipe or request serving guidance or meal ideas, the relevant photo, text, link, ingredients and feeding-stage information are sent to our serverless proxy. OpenAI processes the content needed to read it or generate a response. Do not include names, faces or other personal information in photos or pasted text.

Nearby location. When you use nearby features, location coordinates may be sent to our proxy and Google Places to find and rank nearby places. This is separate from your child's locally stored profile.

Usage events. The app sends product-interaction events to our proxy with a random, persistent installation identifier and a timestamp. Event fields may include verdict state, source, time viewed, feeding stage and counts of foods, recipes or children. These events are written to server logs. The installation identifier does not contain your name or email, but it can connect events from the same installation; it is not fully anonymous.

Subscriptions. Apple processes payments for Tiny Foodie Pro. RevenueCat processes purchase history and subscription information under an app user identifier so access and restore purchases work.

OneSignal, when configured. The app can initialize OneSignal and send a feeding-stage tag when a valid OneSignal app ID is configured. OneSignal may collect installation identifiers and SDK/device metadata. This integration is conditional: having the SDK in the app does not mean it is enabled in every build. The current source configuration uses a placeholder app ID.

Retention and service providers

Our menu-scan cache stores extracted menu answers, keyed by a hash of the submitted photo, for up to 30 days. That cache does not store the raw menu photo. Other menu and place data may be cached to avoid repeating requests. Generated food and recipe images may be stored in our image cache and hosting storage.

OpenAI's normal API abuse-monitoring logs may include prompts, responses and other submitted content and may retain it for up to 30 days, subject to its policies and exceptions. We do not promise zero retention by OpenAI. See OpenAI's data controls.

Providers used for these features include Apple, RevenueCat, OpenAI, Google Places, Vercel (hosting and image storage) and Upstash (cache); OneSignal is used when configured. Provider logs, subscription records and caches are separate from the data stored on your device and follow their applicable retention practices. We use this data to provide app features, manage subscriptions and understand product use, not to sell personal information.

What stays on your device

The full local profile, including name, birth month, saved history and tried-foods/allergen records, is stored with SwiftData. Derived feeding-stage information and event counts can still leave the device as described above. We do not claim that every piece of child-related information stays on your phone.

Children's privacy

Tiny Foodie is for parents and caregivers, not for children to use independently. We do not ask children to create accounts. Parents should avoid submitting identifying child information to the photo, text or recipe-reading features.

Your choices and deletion

You can delete local app data from Settings. Deleting the app removes its local data; it does not automatically erase provider logs, shared caches or purchase records. Manage or cancel subscriptions in your Apple account subscription settings. You can decline camera, photo or location permissions, though the related feature may not work.

For privacy questions or requests about data held by our services, email yazbuilds@gmail.com. We will help identify the relevant records and explain any limits on deletion.

Changes

We will post updates here when our data practices change.
