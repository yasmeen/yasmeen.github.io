FAQ

Where is my baby's data stored?
Your full profile and saved history are stored on your device, with no app account. Scans, recipe imports and nearby features send the information needed to our services and providers; usage events include a persistent installation ID. See the privacy policy for details. Settings deletes local data, not provider logs, caches or purchase records.

How do verdicts work?
A deterministic rules engine built on pediatric feeding guidance - stage bans (honey under 12 months, whole grapes, popcorn, raw fish), first tastes of the big 9 allergens, and preparation changes. The AI only reads menus and answers bounded questions; the rules make the call.

What's free?
Unlimited dish checks, 3 menu scans a week, and 10 saved recipes - forever. Tiny Foodie Pro lifts the limits: $4.99/month or $29.99/year with a 7-day free trial for eligible subscribers. The paywall shows the current prices and allowances.

How do I manage or cancel Pro?
Settings > your Apple ID > Subscriptions, or from the paywall in the app. Restore purchases anytime from Settings.

Feeding guidance

Tiny Foodie is a planning aid, not medical advice or an allergy diagnosis. Menu descriptions and AI readings can miss ingredients. Confirm ingredients with the restaurant, supervise eating, and ask your pediatrician about allergy concerns or feeding readiness.
